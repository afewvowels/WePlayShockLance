Version: 0.3.0
Date: 2026-11-11
  Features:
    - Map settings for plasma orb diameter, plasma orb travel distance, shock combo explosion radius, a 1x to 4x damage multiplier, friendly fire (on by default) and clearing trees and rocks (on by default). They can be changed mid-game.
  Bugfixes:
    - Bolts and orbs now hit big targets like biter spawners anywhere on their footprint instead of only near the centre, so shots no longer pass over the edges of nests.
  Changes:
    - Plasma orbs no longer explode when they touch an enemy; they wink out in a small puff of lavender sparks instead (the contact damage is unchanged).
    - Plasma orbs fly much farther by default (100 tiles, up from about 33) for long-range combos.
    - Bolts now fly at least as far as orbs do, so a bolt can always reach a flying orb.
    - The orb's enemy contact radius and combo catch radius scale with its diameter.
    - Combo visuals, crater, particles and light scale with the explosion radius.
    - With friendly fire on, blasts now also damage friendly buildings and vehicles, not just characters.
    - Shock combos clear trees, rocks and ground decoratives in the blast radius. Nothing drops; trees leave stumps.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version: 0.2.1
Date: 2026-11-11
  Changes:
    - Shock combo and orb blasts now hurt characters caught in the blast radius, including the player who set them off.
  Bugfixes:
    - Bolts and orbs now come out of the gun barrel on the player model: the emission point is lower and slightly closer (measured in game).
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version: 0.2.0
Date: 2026-11-11
  Changes:
    - Alt fire (plasma orb) now defaults to the right mouse button, with G as the alternate key.
    - Primary fire is now a short glowing bolt that travels to its target instead of an instant beam. It hits the first enemy in its path (no longer pierces) and sets off a shock combo when it catches an orb mid-flight.
    - Bolts and orbs now come out of the gun barrel instead of the player's feet.
  Graphics:
    - New supernova-style shock combo explosion: a solid, saturated purple volume, a white-hot core with pink and purple light spikes, a lens-flare streak, a wispy expanding shell, a thin tilted equator ring, sparkly lavender haze and spark particles.
    - The plasma orb now looks like a little plasma-ball toy: a dark glass sphere with animated lightning crawling from a glowing core to the glass, floating sparkles and a glassy highlight.
    - Shock combos leave a scorched crater. It flashes with a white-hot, layered purple bloom that lights up the ground, fades within a few seconds to a quieter crack glow that cools over 8 seconds, and the crater itself fades away after about a minute.
    - Removed the vanilla big explosion from the combo.
  Sounds:
    - Shock combo is louder and bassier, with a sub-bass thump and rumble layered under it, and can be heard from twice as far away.

Version: 0.1.0
Date: 2026-11-11
  Major Features:
    - Initial release.
    - Shock Lance energy rifle with an instant beam on primary fire.
    - Alt-fire hotkey (default G or mouse button 4) launches a slow plasma orb.
    - Shooting an orb with the beam sets off a large shock combo explosion.
    - Powered by armor batteries instead of ammo: 100 kW while selected, 1.5 MJ per beam or orb, 5 MJ per shock combo.
    - Internal 10 MJ capacitor charged from spare suit generator output, so generator-only armor works without batteries.
    - Charge meter in the weapons panel: the bar under the Shock Lance's ammo slot shows suit charge.
    - Glowing purple charge canister icon in the Shock Lance's ammo slot.
    - Custom sound effects for the beam, the orb and the shock combo.
    - Laser damage and laser shooting speed research also apply to the Shock Lance.
